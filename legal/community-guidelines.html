Social FightIn – Community Guidelines

Company: SOCIALFIGHTIN IRELAND t/a Social Fightin

Website: socialfightin.com

Privacy Contact: socialfightin@gmail.com

  1. No harassment or abuse: Do not use the app to send harassment, threats, hate speech, or targeted abuse toward boxers, trainers, promoters, managers, fans, or any other user.
  2. No impersonation or misrepresentation: Do not create fake identities, impersonate athletes, promoters, gyms, sanctioning bodies, or staff, or misrepresent your role, credentials, affiliations, or fight records.
  3. No misinformation: Do not post or share false, misleading, or manipulated information about matchups, rankings, contracts, weigh-ins, fight results, business relationships, or promotional opportunities.
  4. No illegal or fraudulent conduct: Do not use the platform to solicit, arrange, or facilitate illegal activity, including match fixing, bribery, illicit gambling schemes, or other fraudulent conduct related to boxing events or management.
  5. No unauthorized sharing of private or protected content: Do not upload or distribute copyrighted media, private contact details, medical information, contract terms, or other confidential content without proper authorization.

Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or termination under our Content Moderation Policy. To report or appeal, see Reporting & Appeals.
